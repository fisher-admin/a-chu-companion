# Support / 使用帮助

[中文说明](README.md) · [English guide](README.en.md) · [版本记录 / History](CHANGELOG.md)

## 中文

1. **连接或权限提示**：运行正式安装位置的应用；在辅助功能中允许它，点击 Claude 输入框后用实体键盘按 Control＋Option＋E。不要使用另一份临时签名副本。工具每秒检查权限，正常开启无需重启。
2. **回填但未发送**：核对「填入后发送」及发送键。无法验证目标输入时不会自动发送；翻译期间切换焦点、改动输入框或切会话可能需要重新连接。
3. **回复未出现**：看顶部是否为「正在读取」；等待正式回复结束及约三秒稳定检查。保持连接窗口和 Claude 页面打开。滚动显示历史正文后再使用「读取历史回复」。
4. **翻译准备**：先确认选定语言包可用，首次使用可能显示原生下载提示。取消或失败会保留原文，按提示重试；不要卸载语言包来解决一般读取问题。
5. **额度未知／旧数据**：检查连接来源是否属于当前聊天账户。桌面和网页账户不同时连接对应 session。登录失效时重新登录或更换 session，不要把凭据发到 Issue。
6. **长内容**：中文输入上限 10,000，外语上限 50,000 字符；超限请缩短。执行代码核对原文，翻译可能改变标点。
7. **升级后授权**：正常同身份升级复用权限。换电脑、签名身份或撤销权限需要重新允许。不要删除原签名证书或重置其他应用授权。

仍有问题时 [提交问题表单](https://github.com/fisher-admin/a-chu-companion/issues/new/choose)，附版本、系统、客户端、复现步骤及模拟截图。未验证范围包括其他浏览器、Intel Mac、macOS 15 实际往返、最小化／冻结网页的组合；不要把这些场景当成完整支持承诺。

## English

Use the normally installed app and grant Accessibility permission. Click Claude's composer, then connect with physical Control+Option+E. Avoid temporary-signed copies. Permission status updates without a restart.

If insertion succeeds but sending does not, check the auto-send option and keyboard setting. Unverifiable delivery is not automatically sent. Changed focus, composer contents, or conversation may require reconnecting.

For missing replies, check that reading is active, wait for completion and roughly three seconds of stable text, and keep the connected window/page open. Reveal historical text before requesting history. Initial translation may require downloading a language pack; cancellation and errors preserve the original for retry.

Usage must belong to the chat account. Reconnect expired sessions; never include them in issues. Chinese and foreign-text limits are 10,000 and 50,000 characters respectively. Check the original before executing translated code.

Normal same-identity updates can reuse permissions; changed identities/computers or revoked permission may require authorization. Do not delete the original signing certificate or reset permissions for unrelated apps.

[Open a sanitized issue](https://github.com/fisher-admin/a-chu-companion/issues/new/choose) with version, environment, and reproduction details. Other browsers, Intel Macs, actual macOS 15 round trips, and minimized/suspended-page combinations have not received equivalent validation.
