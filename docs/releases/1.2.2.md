# A畜伴侣 1.2.2（build82） / AChu Companion 1.2.2

## 中文

A畜伴侣让你在一个窗口里用中文写消息、看 Claude 的中文回复。连接目标输入框并开启「填入后发送」后，中文会自动翻译为所选外语、填入并发送；回复原文先显示，稳定段落随后及时译回中文。默认使用 macOS 系统翻译，可选 Gemini 或兼容 AI 翻译服务。

## English

AChu Companion keeps Chinese composition and Chinese translations of Claude replies in one window. Connect the target input and enable **Send after filling** to translate, paste and send automatically. Original replies appear first, with stable paragraphs translated promptly. macOS system translation is the default; Gemini and compatible AI translators are optional.
